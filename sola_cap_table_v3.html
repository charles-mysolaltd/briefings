Analysis
Key observations
Founder control is comfortable in all scenarios. Charles holds above 80% if no milestones are hit, and stays well above 75% even when all milestones are fully earned. The 75% special resolution threshold is secure throughout.
Sunil's combined stake reflects two separate grants. His 4 milestone shares (issued now) plus his 2 cash shares (issued at Round 1) give him 6 shares total — but they dilute differently because the cash shares enter a larger pool. His final percentage varies by milestone scenario; toggle through the outcomes above to see each case.
Al's milestones must be defined before the shareholders' agreement is signed. Sunil's milestone triggers have a natural shape tied to fundraising. Al's are not yet specified. The forfeiture schedule is unenforceable without specific, measurable, time-bound triggers. This is the one outstanding item before any documents are executed.
Round 1 share price is slightly lower than a clean 100-share model. Because Sunil and Al's shares exist before Round 1 opens, the pre-money valuation is divided across 108 shares rather than 100. At £1m pre-money this produces a price of £9,259 per share rather than £10,000. For investors this is immaterial; the valuation is what they're buying into, not the share price.
